Its place in the chain

What it showsWhat the author's terminal said about one ticker, the price and the index level at that instant, and what happened on each fixed anniversary.
What the chain addsEach mark's hash sits in a chain whose head is posted daily to a clock the author does not control. A server head that counts past this mark's sequence number means the mark existed by that day.
What it does not proveThe whole chain. For that, ask the author for the evidence pack and check it on the verify page.
What it is notA recommendation. It is a record of what was said under rules the author set.

Source line: the record kept by PerceptFolio for its author; prices as recorded at the call and at each mark; the index is the S&P 500 through SPY.
