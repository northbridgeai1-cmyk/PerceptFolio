PerceptFolio icon generator

iOS will not accept an SVG for a home-screen icon, so these have to be real PNG files. Everything is drawn locally in this page — nothing is uploaded anywhere.

If your browser asks permission to download multiple files, allow it. Otherwise use the individual buttons.

What to do with them

  1. Upload all four PNGs to your GitHub repo, alongside index.html.
  2. Upload the updated index.html and manifest.json too.
  3. On your iPhone: delete the old PerceptFolio icon from your home screen first. iOS caches the icon at the moment you add it and will not refresh it on its own.
  4. Open perceptfolio.com in Safari again, Share → Add to Home Screen.
