Privacy Notice

Document PF-PRIV-001 Effective 5 September 2026 Version 1.0 Operator NorthBridge, financial branch

This Notice explains what personal data is processed in connection with PerceptFolio (the "Service"), on what basis, and what rights the individual has in respect of it. It is issued by NorthBridge's financial branch ("NorthBridge", "we") as the controller of that data.

It has been drafted from the operating source code rather than adapted from a precedent, and each statement of fact in it is verifiable against that code.

1.Definitions

Service
The PerceptFolio research terminal published at perceptfolio.com/terminal, together with the associated landing page and access-request facility.
Local Data
Portfolio holdings, cash balances, watchlists, lists, written theses, configured rules and the record of graded calls, held in the browser's localStorage on the User's own device.
Worker
The Cloudflare Worker operated by NorthBridge which provides the access queue, invite validation, optional synchronisation and data proxying.
User
An individual to whom access has been granted, or an applicant who has submitted an access request.

2.Summary of position

Local Data does not leave the User's browser unless synchronisation is enabled by the User. No cookies are set. No analytics, tag management, session recording, fingerprinting or advertising technology is employed. NorthBridge personnel cannot view a User's holdings.

3.Data held on the User's device

3.1 The Service executes wholly within the User's browser. Local Data is written to that browser's localStorage and is not transmitted by default.

3.2 Account credentials create a profile in the same storage. The password is hashed using SHA-256 before storage and is not transmitted at any point. No server-side account exists and, accordingly, no password reset facility can be offered.

Notice

Clearing browser storage destroys Local Data irrecoverably. NorthBridge holds no copy and cannot restore it. Safari deletes site storage after seven days without a visit. Users are advised to export a backup, and the Service prompts them to do so.

5.Data held by NorthBridge

5.1 The Worker retains the following categories and no others:

  1. Access requests. The email address and description submitted, any optional written call, together with the IP address and user-agent string, the latter two being retained solely to enforce rate limiting.
  2. Invite codes and grants. The code issued, its tier, and whether it has been redeemed or suspended.
  3. Call registries and marks. Ticker, date and entry price of calls awaiting a scheduled mark. No holdings, share counts or cash balances.
  4. Synchronisation data. Where enabled by the User, Local Data as submitted, stored under a key derived from the account.

6.Retention and erasure

6.1 Local Data is erased on deletion of the profile within the Service, or on clearing of browser storage. Such erasure is immediate and complete.

6.2 Invite codes expire forty days after issue. A record of a grant is retained for the life of the account. Access requests, including those refused, are retained until deleted by the operator.

6.3 Synchronisation data is overwritten on each synchronisation and is erased on request.

6.4 A request for erasure of data held by NorthBridge should be sent from the address to which the request relates. Requests are handled by a person.

7.Rights of the individual

7.1 A complete export of all data held by the Service is available to the User at any time from the Settings screen, in machine-readable form. That export is the entirety of the data, not a summary.

7.2 Where the General Data Protection Regulation or the UK GDPR applies, the rights of access, rectification, erasure, restriction and portability apply accordingly. The facility at clause 7.1 satisfies the rights of access and portability without application to NorthBridge.

8.Amendment

8.1 Any amendment altering what data leaves the User's browser will be recorded in this Notice together with its date.

8.2 The version and effective date appear at the head of this document.
