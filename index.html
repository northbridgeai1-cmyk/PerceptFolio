Scan a list of tickers

QuantFolio doesn't browse the market on its own — give it a list and it scores each ticker against your checklist. Scoring takes a few seconds per ticker to stay within Finnhub's free-tier rate limit, so this runs in the background; you can leave the tab and come back.

Presets are a fixed snapshot, not a live index feed — membership can drift over time. Or just paste your own list below.
